# Northwind Smart Thermostat — Product Guide

## Installation

The Northwind Smart Thermostat works with most 24V heating and cooling systems, including gas furnaces, heat pumps and central air conditioning. Installation takes about 30 minutes. Turn off power at the breaker, label the existing wires with the stickers in the box, mount the Northwind base plate, connect the wires to the matching terminals and restore power. The app then walks you through Wi-Fi setup and your first schedule.

## Schedules and learning

During the first week the thermostat records when you raise or lower the temperature and builds a schedule automatically. You can edit the schedule at any time in the app, set separate weekday and weekend programmes, and add holiday periods. Eco mode lowers heating or raises cooling targets while nobody is home.

## Troubleshooting

If the display is blank, check the breaker and make sure the C-wire is connected. If the thermostat shows "No Wi-Fi", restart the router, then hold the menu button for five seconds to rejoin the network. If heating runs constantly, check that the W1 wire is not touching the Rh terminal. Firmware updates install overnight automatically; you can also start one from Settings, About, Check for updates.
