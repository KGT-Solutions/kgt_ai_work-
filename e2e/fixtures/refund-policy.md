# Northwind Return & Refund Policy

## Returns and refunds

You can return any Northwind thermostat within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, for any reason. The product must include its base plate and wiring stickers. Start a return from the app under Settings, Orders, or email returns@northwind.example with your order number.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds are paid to the original payment method within seven business days of the returned unit reaching our Pune warehouse. Shipping for returns is free inside India; we email a prepaid label within one business day of your request.

## Exceptions

Units damaged by incorrect wiring are not eligible for a refund but are repaired at cost. Business plan subscriptions can be cancelled at any time; the unused months of a yearly plan are refunded pro rata.
