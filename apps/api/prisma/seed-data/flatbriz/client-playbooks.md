## Talking to Builders

<!-- tags: client:builder, benefit:instant_pwa_install, benefit:fast_migration -->
Builders typically evaluate FLATBRIZ to hand over a ready-made society-management setup to the
first RWA/committee once a project is possession-ready, or to offer it as a value-add across
multiple projects. The relevant angle: FLATBRIZ can be configured per building/tower before
handover, and because it's a PWA, there's no app-store rollout lag when onboarding a large number
of new owners at once — the Building Code and a shared link get residents in from day one.
Builders managing multiple projects benefit from being able to stand up a new building's setup
quickly and repeat the same configuration across towers/phases.

## Talking to RWA Presidents

<!-- tags: client:rwa_president, benefit:zero_gateway_commission, benefit:transparent_ledger -->
RWA presidents are usually most persuaded by cost and transparency: zero gateway commission on
maintenance collections directly protects the corpus, and a transparent per-flat ledger reduces
the disputes and "did I really pay?" questions that eat into a president's time. Lead with the
financial angle, then bring in visitor management, complaints, and announcements as the
operational features that come bundled in rather than requiring a separate tool.

## Talking to Committee Members

<!-- tags: client:committee_member, benefit:transparent_ledger, benefit:corpus_fund_management -->
Committee members (often treasurers or secretaries) want to know the tool will make their specific
job easier and won't create more admin work than it saves. Emphasize automatic bill generation,
the corpus fund tracking, and the audit trail on the ledger — these directly reduce the manual
spreadsheet work a treasurer currently does every month, and give them something concrete and
credible to present at the AGM.

## General Discovery Questions to Ask

Good questions for any prospect, whatever their role: approximate flat
count, what tool (if any) is used today, how maintenance is currently collected (cash, bank
transfer, gateway), and whether there's a specific pain point (settlement lag, disputes,
spreadsheet upkeep) prompting the search for something new. These answers let the next
conversation (with the human sales team) start from the prospect's actual situation instead of a
generic pitch.
