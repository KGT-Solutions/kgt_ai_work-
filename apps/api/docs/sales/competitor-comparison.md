# FLATBRIZ vs. MyGate vs. NoBroker

Comparison talking points and objection handling. Keep comparisons factual and specific to what's
written here — never invent a competitor feature or pricing figure that isn't stated.

## FLATBRIZ vs. MyGate

<!-- tags: competitor:mygate, benefit:zero_gateway_commission, benefit:lower_total_cost -->
MyGate is widely known for visitor/gate management and is a solid product there, but its
maintenance-collection flows typically route through a payment gateway that takes a commission on
every transaction. FLATBRIZ's UPI-first collection has zero gateway commission on maintenance
payments, and pairs that with the same category of gate/visitor management (Visitor tab,
pre-approved Gate Passes, guard logging) FLATBRIZ residents and guards already use — so a society
isn't trading away visitor management to get better finance tooling.

## FLATBRIZ vs. NoBroker

<!-- tags: competitor:nobroker, benefit:transparent_ledger, benefit:flexible_expense_splitting -->
NoBroker's society product bundles many services, which can mean less flexibility in how an
individual society's maintenance and expense-splitting rules are configured, since the platform is
built to standardize across many societies. FLATBRIZ lets a building admin configure splitting
rules (equal, by area/BHK, or per-expense-type) to match the society's own AGM-approved bylaws,
and keeps the per-flat ledger transparent and directly visible to residents rather than something
they have to request from the committee.

## Objection: "Payment gateway / settlement lag is a pain with our current tool"

<!-- tags: objection:payment_gateway_lag, benefit:proof_based_collections, benefit:zero_gateway_commission -->
This is one of the most common complaints about gateway-routed collection: money can take days to
settle, and reconciliation against the right flat is manual. FLATBRIZ's UPI collection posts the
payment straight to the flat's ledger with the UPI transaction reference as proof, and because
there's no gateway commission step in the flow, there's no extra settlement hop introducing lag.
Ask the prospect how many days their current settlement takes today, so the contrast is concrete.

## Objection: "Our residents (especially senior citizens) will resist adopting a new app"

<!-- tags: objection:adoption_friction, benefit:instant_pwa_install -->
Adoption friction is usually about the install step, not the app itself. Because FLATBRIZ is a
PWA, there's no app-store search, no account-creation wall — residents tap a link (or scan a QR
with the Building Code) and "Add to Home Screen" takes seconds. For societies worried about
less tech-comfortable residents, recommend a short one-page printed guide at the security desk and
a launch announcement posted the week the building admin gets access — most friction is a
one-time onboarding moment, not an ongoing usability problem.

## Objection: "We're still on Excel/WhatsApp — migration feels risky"

<!-- tags: objection:spreadsheet_migration, benefit:fast_migration, benefit:dedicated_support -->
Migration doesn't require reconstructing years of history inside FLATBRIZ. The society's current
flat list and any outstanding dues are set up once as opening balances, and billing continues from
the next cycle onward — the committee doesn't need to re-key historical transactions. FLATBRIZ
provides hands-on onboarding support for this setup step specifically because it's the part
societies worry about most.

## Objection: "This sounds more expensive than what we use now"

<!-- tags: objection:pricing, benefit:zero_gateway_commission, benefit:lower_total_cost -->
Compare total cost, not just the subscription line: if the current tool takes a payment-gateway
commission on every maintenance collection, that recurring cost often outweighs a platform fee over
a year, especially for larger societies with high monthly collection volume. FLATBRIZ's zero
gateway commission on UPI collections is the concrete number to walk through with the prospect —
ask for their approximate monthly collection amount so the commission-savings comparison is real,
not hypothetical.
